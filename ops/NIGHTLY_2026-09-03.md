### 2a. The 09:32 manual SELL disabled the auto detector for the whole day

`day_rider.py:1377` — the once-per-session latch is `if st.get("entered") or st.get("closed")`.
Its own comment says it exists "to stop the DETECTOR re-entering", but `do_manual_entry`
(line 818) sets `entered=True` on the same flag. So the 09:32Z hand-press consumed the session's
single-entry budget, and from **13:30:28Z — the first tick of the automatic window — through
20:39Z the rider printed "already traded this session — no re-entry" (209 ticks)**. The
13:30–15:00Z detector window never got a look at the tape.

Not obviously wrong (the operator was clearly trading it by hand all day), but it is a silent
side effect of a button that reads as "add a position", and it is invisible unless you read the
note field. **Decision needed:** should a manual press before 13:30Z burn the detector's session?

### 2b. An out-of-RTH manual entry gets `arm_atr = 0` and silently falls back to the 150pt arm

`drift.read()` (drift.py:151) returns a blank `DriftRead` outside 13:30–21:00Z. `do_manual_entry`
stores `entry_atr=round(rr.atr,2), arm_atr=round(rr.atr,2)` — so a press outside RTH books
**both as 0.0**. `trail_level()`/the arm test are guarded by `USE_ATR_TRAIL and arm_atr and
arm_atr > 0`, so the ATR trail is skipped and the arm reverts to the fixed `ARM_PT = 150`.

Observed today on the 09:32Z SHORT: 37 consecutive ticks of
`riding · trail not armed (need +150 [fixed], at -0)`. A 4-lot MNQ scalp will not travel 150pt;
that position rode 34 minutes with **no reachable trail at all** and was saved only by the
operator's claim at +9pt. Every in-RTH entry today read `need +25 [4xATR 6.2]` — the intended
behaviour — so the fallback is invisible in the common case.

The manual path advertises itself as live "across the whole CME session"
(`day_rider_state.json.note`), which is precisely the window where this fires.

### 2c. Cosmetic: stale `lots_open` after a whole-position exit

### 3a. ★ The router's `day_rider` figure is a 6-row tail sum, not a day total

The 22:40Z tick reads *"TOURNAMENT day +0.0 (day_rider -686 is clientId 4, not my book)"*.
The rider's actual desk day is **-$372.00**. -686 is exactly the four rider rows visible in
`scripts/recent_trades.py 6`: -245 -356 -150 +65. The router summed the RECENT TRADES tail and
narrated it as a day figure — an **84% overstatement** of the second desk's loss.

Why the real number was unavailable: `desk_view.py` keys its day P&L on **`opened_at >= ds`**
while `pnl.py` — the desk's single P&L source — keys the day on **`closed_at`**. After the
22:00Z rollover no rider trade has `opened_at >= ds`, so the `— day_rider` line disappears from
DESK VIEW entirely and the only rider number left in the router's context is the truncated tail.

Consequence today: none — the rider is context the router does not route. But the router is
graded from its stated reasons, and a fabricated-looking -686 in the ledger is a bad-call review
that cannot be reconciled.

The `opened_at` vs `closed_at` basis mismatch is otherwise near-harmless: **1 of 833 trades**
straddles a Paris boundary — the 08-21→08-24 weekend rider hold, -$2,149.44.

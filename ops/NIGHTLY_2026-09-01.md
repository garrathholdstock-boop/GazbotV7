## Decisions needed

1. **The rider has no absolute-loss alert.** #805 lost $1,466 over nine hours in total silence. Every
   existing alert is keyed to *giveback from a peak*, so a trade that is never profitable can never
   trigger one. This is the second observation of the pattern.
2. **A manual entry outside 13:30–21:00Z gets `arm_atr = 0`, a frozen excursion and no stop.** Either
   refuse manual entries outside the drift window, or give `drift.read` a fallback price/ATR source
   for out-of-hours so the manage path is not blind. Right now the rider's protections are all
   silently disabled exactly when a human overrides it.
3. **`desk_reconcile.py` orphan check should compare against the owning clientId's book**, not the
   account net.
4. **The `cx_clip` arm is inert** (3 byte-identical variants) — fix or retire.
5. ⚠ **DST, from 2026-10-25.** Paris goes to UTC+1, the desk boundary/reopen moves 22:00Z → 23:00Z,
   and this job stays pinned at 22:43 UTC — it would fire **17 min before** the reopen instead of 43
   min after, and report the last complete desk day (one behind what the operator expects). The
   query above stays correct either way; **the timer is what needs moving, to ~23:43 UTC.** Do not
   fudge the window to compensate.

## Durable rule

**A blind price read disables a strategy's stop, its trail bar and its alerting at once — check what
a degraded read *silently zeroes*, not just what it returns.** `drift.read` returning blank
out-of-hours froze the excursion at +0 (so no giveback alert could arm), set `arm_atr = 0` (so the
trail bar fell back to the widest fixed floor) and left `venue_stop` null — three independent
protections, one cause, $1,466.

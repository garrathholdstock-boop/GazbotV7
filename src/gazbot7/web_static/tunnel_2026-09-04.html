What you are looking at
Solid yellow — a tunnel: a quiet run of ≥25 minutes. The box top and bottom are the ceiling and floor.
Pale yellow — a shorter quiet run, below the 25-minute floor. Shown so you can see what the threshold excludes.
Dashed lines — the buffer, ±10% of the tunnel's width.
Green circle — the first minute closing beyond the buffer, up or down.
Grey band — 00–07Z Asia, where the desk is entry-blocked by config.

Close, not a wick, deliberately: on 2026-09-03 at 16:24Z a close 0.75pt outside satisfied a naive “outside” test and meant nothing — the real move came a minute later, 5.8pt out on 4,222 lots.

Only 17.5% of breaks fire in US hours; 35.8% fire inside the benched Asia block. A ≥25-minute compression that breaks is mostly an overnight event. Whatever the setup turns out to be worth, most instances arrive when standing policy says do not enter — so the tradeable population is far smaller than the raw count suggests.

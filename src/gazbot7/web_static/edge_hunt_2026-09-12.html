1 The answer

No. We did not find an edge. Thirty-six hypotheses went in — momentum, reversion, scalp, all-day-hold, volatility, order book, portfolio construction — and thirty-six came out refuted. One of them, the European-open drift window, cleared every one of its own pre-registered kill conditions and was carried forward as a survivor; three independent teams were then pointed at it with instructions to break it, and all three did, on separate grounds. The final count of things I would put money on is zero.

I am not going to dress that up. There is no rule in this report that you should trade on Monday, and the honest reason is not that we looked in the wrong places. It is that we now have a very good measurement of how big an edge would have to be before this desk could both profit from it and prove it exists — and nothing we found is within a factor of two of that size.

What the hunt did produce is real, just not a trade. Volatility is genuinely forecastable on this tape and direction is not, confirmed four separate ways. Gold is not the diversifier we have been assuming. And there is about $4,000 a year of pure friction sitting in how the desk places its orders, which has nothing to do with finding a signal at all.

Out of sample, on data nobody had touched. What it is worth per year on one lot at real cost. The author said $1,374 [$390, $2,360]. The honest re-estimate after the cost lens is $0 to $500/yr, CI spanning zero, and contingent on two or three macro crashes recurring on schedule. Do not trade it.

What it would take to promote it past shadow. It cannot be promoted. The block that carries it (03:00–03:29 ET) was not registered, so testing it now is a new hypothesis, not a promotion — and it would need roughly two more years of our own lake before the conditioner's standard error is small enough to settle even the sign.

Volatility is roughly 100× more forecastable than direction on this tape. That is the single most solid quantitative fact the hunt produced.

And it does not convert into money. Sizing by 1/σ̂ moves Sharpe by +0.0029, CI [−0.0123, +0.0177], and a randomly scrambled forecast sizes just as well (p = 0.45) — because the RTH risk premium is proportional to forecast volatility, so dividing exposure by σ divides the mean and the standard deviation by the same number. Gating on it made the desk worse in 13 of 15 cells. Using it as a router affordability floor captured −2.4% of what a perfect oracle was worth.

So it is a stop-width and risk-budget input — not a lot-count input, and not a gate. Refit per instrument: the NQ coefficients frozen onto gold score −0.210, i.e. worse than nothing.

6 The next dig

The one direction left that we have not actually tried is BREADTH — and it is the only lever whose arithmetic beats the wall.

Here is why it is the answer and nothing else is. The structural wall says the smallest per-trade edge that reaches t = 2 on one instrument is 2.84 pt at a 5-minute hold, against a gross ceiling of 1.05–1.50 pt. Duration escapes the fee and escapes statistical power at the same √ rate, so they cancel — you cannot hold your way out. The brief named two levers: sample and breadth. This hunt spent itself on sample: 10.5 years, 3.7 million minutes, 2,011 configurations, every one charged against its own null world. Sample is done, and it returned zero. Breadth is untouched — 35 of the 36 hypotheses tested one instrument, and the 36th tested K=2 using the two most correlated things on the board.

portfolio Sharpe        S  = s · DR

At ρ = 0.31, eight markets is a 1.62× multiplier on whatever per-market edge exists — and critically, n grows without paying the fee more often, because each market takes one decision. That is the only structure in this whole problem where the statistics and the economics move in the same direction instead of cancelling.

What it needs, concretely — about two days and $0

  1. Land the daily data. scripts/fetch_yahoo_daily.py already exists. 28 roots, ~25 years of front-month daily OHLCV. Gate: ≥20 roots with ≥15 years. If Yahoo stays blocked, one month of Norgate is ~$70.

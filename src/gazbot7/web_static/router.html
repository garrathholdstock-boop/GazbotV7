Why "chop" when the dashboard shows ER 0.30–0.50? Those are different clocks. The 30-min ER is high because each intraday leg is a clean directional half-hour push — but the legs alternate and cancel, so over the session price round-trips inside its band → day-ER stays near 0.03. That is chop: real short legs that net nowhere. It's why "clean trend hours" keep faking, and why momentum gates buy the top / sell the bottom of a leg right before it rotates. The router runs momentum only when the legs sustain, not when they alternate.

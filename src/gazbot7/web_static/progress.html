NET is the column to read — it is what the account moved by, and it is the convention every other section uses. NET is sum(pnl_usd) straight off the book, because pnl_usd is recorded already net of fees; GROSS is reconstructed by adding fees_usd back at $1.50 per round trip per lot. Do not subtract the fee from pnl_usd — that is the mistake the previous revision of this page made, and it cost $2,337.00 of imaginary loss.

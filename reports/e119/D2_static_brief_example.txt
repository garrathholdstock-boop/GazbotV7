HOW HE TRADES (confirmation, never anticipation)
  He joins a major intraday leg that has already proven itself, in its direction. He never calls the turn and never enters against the
  leg that is running. "I watch that the leg has turned and is grinding up or down the other way and I jump in."
  He dislikes buying the top of a run just before it turns. But on the desk's record "being at the top" does not pick out the losers -
  winners and losers both entered at fresh highs/lows, in long legs, after big bars. So judge whether the leg is still PROGRESSING, not
  where price sits in it.

REASONS TO WAIT THAT THE DESK'S RECORD SUPPORTS
  - The leg is old and has stopped making progress: little net move in its direction for an hour or more.
  - Little time is left before the US cash open (see LIVE STATE): an entry that has not paid by then rides the US session.
  A fresh high or low, a long leg, or a big last bar are NOT on their own reasons to wait. And no reason to wait is ever, once absent,
  a reason to enter: enter because the leg is driving, not because nothing on a list is wrong.

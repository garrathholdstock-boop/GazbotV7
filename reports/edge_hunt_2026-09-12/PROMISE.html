4. The structural finding, and why it governs everything above

On 239 sessions the smallest per-trade edge that reaches statistical significance is 2.84 points at a 5-minute hold, rising to 23.57 points at a full-session hold — against a gross edge ceiling in this instrument of 1.05 to 1.50 points. Holding longer escapes the fixed fee and escapes statistical power at the same rate, so they cancel exactly.

On one year of one instrument there is no holding period at which this desk can both profit and prove it. That is not a reason to stop; it is the reason the answer keeps coming back "not proven" and why the bar for shadow must be lower than the bar for money.

Breadth is the one lever that beats this, and it was confirmed at theory today: 14 markets at an average strategy correlation of +0.099 give 6.1 effective bets and a realised multiplier of 2.27× against a predicted 2.47×. The mechanism works. It is multiplying a per-market Sharpe of 0.13 into 0.39, and vol-targeted buy-and-hold on the same markets does 0.54 — so breadth is real, measurable, and on this evidence still too small. It also requires multi-week overnight holds.

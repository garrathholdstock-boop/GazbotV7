Cause of death, proven ex-ante. I took the 13 run starts and asked where each candidate feature sat inside 6,000 random live bars. Position in the 2-min range: 53rd percentile, p=1.000 — which kills the "fade at the extreme" premise outright. Absorption (points per contract): 50th percentile — no absorption signature at all. Far-side book depth: 48th percentile — there is no liquidity vacuum in the book. The only "significant" feature is the signed 60-second flow itself, at the 22nd percentile — which is tautological, because VACUUM is defined as opposite flow, and it points the wrong way. Nothing measurable at the trigger separates the 13 runs from the ~500 identical-looking setups that go nowhere.

Step 5 — THE SURVIVOR: NIPC, the news-impulse pullback (full level, OPEN/NEWS)

Here is where the detective story turns. The prior week buried a Donchian breakout in this exact window, and that grave is what made this week's hunt worth doing properly: it says chasing fails here, which is a fact about the tape, not a verdict on the cluster. So before inventing anything I labelled every 5-second bar in the 13:00–15:00 window across 12 days (17,274 observations) with backward-only features and the forward 15-minute payoff. Two facts reframed the whole problem:

The mechanical spec — this is the deliverable. Armed only 13:00 ≤ t < 15:00 UTC. Decisions on 5-second bar closes, fills on ticks. Direction is always with the impulse; both sides always armed; no flow or book input at all.

StepRule
1. IMPULSEOver the trailing 2 minutes (24 bars), leg = close[i] − close[i−24]. Require |leg| ≥ 1.0 × ATR1m measured before the impulse (no self-reference). Side = sign(leg). ext = the impulse extreme, org = its origin, span = |ext − org|, require span ≥ 5 pt.
2. PULLBACKWalk forward up to 3 minutes tracking the retrace r = |ext − pullback| / span. If r > 0.75 → ABANDON (structure broken, that was not a pullback). ARM at the first bar where r ≥ 0.35.
3. TRIGGEREntry level = half-back of the pullback (pullback extreme + 0.50 × the retrace distance), and it must still be inside the impulse. Live for 1 minute; fill at the first tick trading through it. No fill → the setup expires, no trade.
4. STOPThe pullback extreme ± a 4-point buffer. R = |entry − stop|. Reject the setup if R < 2 pt. (Median R in practice = 17.2 pt.)
5. EXIT1 lot: fixed 2.5R target. Hard 20-minute time cap; flat by 15:30 UTC. Two-lot desk architecture: Lot A 2.0R / Lot B 2.5R (proven, below).
6. RISKOne position at a time; 2-minute cooldown after exit.
7. REGIMEOFF in dead-chop (ATR1m < 18 pt AND ER15 < 0.35). ON in every other regime.

Why this is a different animal, not a filtered breakout. One ablation settles it — strip the pullback requirement (RMIN=0, RES=0) and NIPC collapses into a plain impulse-chase: −$2,143 at 27.9% win over 290 trades. Same impulse detector, same regime gate, same exit, same tape, same costs. The pullback-and-resume structure is the edge; it is the opposite trade at the opposite price, not a filter bolted onto a breakout.

Where NIPC fails, stated up front. All four red runs (07-28 14:02, 07-29 13:19, 07-31 13:20, 07-31 14:34) are first legs that reversed inside the run window — the impulse was real, the pullback held, and the 15-minute "run" then completed in the other direction after NIPC had been stopped and re-entered. That is the residual failure mode, and the repair is a one-parameter change: a second-entry-in-the-same-direction veto, or an entry-count cap per impulse cluster.

Shadow NIPC, do not arm it. n=171 over 12 days is one regime-fortnight. There is no cross-regime tape here: no FOMC week, no CPI shock, and — critically — barely any genuinely quiet news window (dead-chop is only 34 trades, and that is precisely the bucket the gate turns off). The desk's own promotion ladder says shadow → 1 lot, gated on 8–12 weeks and cross-regime evidence. I am not going to be the one who short-circuits it on a fortnight.

The claim to re-test first is the EXIT, not the entry. "Fixed 2.5R beats a trail in the news window" contradicts our standing chandelier prior and is worth ~$1,285 in the sweep. If that flips on fresh tape, the whole exit half of the spec flips with it.

Fix the census, not the desk. The FLOW-LED / VACUUM rule fires on 66.5% of all bars. It should be a z-score against a trailing 2 hours with a real threshold (|z| ≥ 1), otherwise the two clusters are two names for one coin and next Friday's greenfield agent digs this same empty hole. That is a one-line fix in scripts/run_census.py::cluster() and it is the highest-value change in this section.

The one stone still worth turning: run the chop-scalp harness on MES, 2026-07-12 → 07-17. The L2 lead did not fail because absorption is a bad idea — it failed because MNQ's book is too thin for absorption to be a measurable quantity. On MES the same construct is a real physical measurement (23.9 lots at the top, 527 across 10-deep, 6.8×/6.1× MNQ). The window exists and the blocker is known: alphabot2/data/depth.db holds 10-deep MES book for 07-12→07-31 and alphabot2/data/ticks.db holds MES prints for 07-05→07-17 — they overlap on 07-12→07-17, roughly 4–5 session days with both a tape and a book. Everything is parameterised and re-runnable.

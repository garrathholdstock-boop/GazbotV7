Method & limits — read before quoting any number above

Venue truth vs model. Every figure in fact tables 1, 2, 4 and 5 is straight from data/gazbot7.db trades — real fills, fees included, nothing modelled. Fact tables 3 and 6 are reprices on 250 ms ticks from data/capture.db with $1.50 round-turn fees, fills assumed at the exact stop/target level and no slippage. Our own execution work says live stops fill 1.1–3.1× worse than modelled, so every reprice number is a ceiling; use them to rank configs against each other, never as a P&L promise.

Sample size. 194 automated fills over six sessions. Regime buckets: violent-whipsaw 81, clean-trend 64, in-between 40, normal-chop 9, dead-chop 0. The sweeps use 64 (exhaustion), 42 (abs_veto_long) and 22 (abs_veto_short) entry events over nine, six and four days. Anything below n≈15 in this section is flagged inline and should be treated as a direction, not a measurement.

Known modelling weakness. 17 of the 64 exhaustion replays never hit a stop, target or trail inside the 120-minute / session-flat cap and are marked at the last tick — the same 17 entries in every config, so they do not tilt one config against another, but they do mean roughly a quarter of the sweep is marked-to-market rather than exited. Separately, an entry that hits its target before the tape later runs through its stop is scored as a win (correctly — the order of events is respected), which is why trade #444 in fact table 3 shows a target despite the tape eventually running 85 pt against it.

Not swept, and it should be. grind_long’s 2.5 / wide pair was not put through this ladder — it is the largest automated loser on the desk (−$421.5) and it has the two graze-sensitive counterfactuals in fact table 3. That sweep is the obvious next job and its absence is a hole in this section, not a silence.

Reconciliation. Week bounds and totals match Part 1 exactly (206 fills, +$625.0); the day-by-day rows here use the UTC close-date rather than Part 1’s Paris session boundary, which moves trades between days but not the week total or any gate total.

3 · ★ The test, and the verdict on all five floors

The test is simple and it is the right one. Not trading costs nothing, so a floor only earns its keep if the population it BLOCKS reliably loses money. "Reliably" means three things at once: the blocked book is net negative, it is still negative after you strip its three biggest winners, and it is negative on the worst leave-one-day-out fold. A floor whose blocked book is positive is not a filter — it is a leak.

7 · The biggest risk, and what this does NOT prove

The biggest risk if you ship all three tonight is that grind_long is a high-count, low-margin book being asked to survive real fills. Even the best config — row E — has a break-even loss multiplier of 1.64, and memory [[shadow-sim-understates-losses]] puts live losses at 1.1–3.1× modelled. That number sits inside the band. If live losses on this gate run at 1.7× rather than 1.5×, the entire +$3,095 is zero, and everything in §5 becomes an argument about noise. The ceiling helps (it is why row E survives 1.5× where row A does not) but it does not lift grind out of the band. Grind is being made less bad and more robust, not made good.

Behind that, three more:

  • My replay is always-armed; the live desk is not. In the same window the router benched 465 grind/abs_veto signal episodes. The interaction between the router's benching and the new floors is unmeasured — the two filters could stack and starve the gate, or overlap and do nothing. Watch the combined block rate on the first session, not just the floor's.
  • Eight days, one regime. Chop-dominated, MNQ-only, PAPER. CLEAN-TREND is n=1 for grind at the ceiling — completely unscored. A wide ext_atr is exactly what a trend day pays for, and ext_hi 2.0 caps it. If Monday opens trending, Step 3 is the first thing to question.
  • Two population caveats. capitulation_long's proxy runs require_flip=False against the live True, so its rows are directional only. And abs_veto_short's ATR verdict is a coin-flip I have called by rule rather than by evidence — I would not defend it hard either way.

What would change my mind: for grind, one clean trend week with ext_atr above 2.0 paying — that is the single scenario this tape cannot speak to. For the two ER deletions, nothing on this evidence; they are the most robust calls in the report, positive on both price bases and all eight folds.

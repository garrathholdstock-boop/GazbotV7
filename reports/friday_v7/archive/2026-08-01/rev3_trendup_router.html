7 · The asymmetry, honestly — and the thing that actually matters this week

The standing rule is that wrongly-benched is cheap (missed trades) and wrongly-armed is expensive (real churn losses), so when unsure, sit benched one more tick. This change points the expensive way. Everything about the evidence says respect the asymmetry:

Verdict: STAGE IT, do not ship it. A change that arms a churner in more conditions, justified by a segment that inverts when you change the definition of the segment, on a week with no confirmed trend in it, is precisely the trade the asymmetry rule exists to refuse. The cost of waiting is a modelled few hundred dollars a week that may not exist; the cost of being wrong is measured, repeatedly, in the ledger.

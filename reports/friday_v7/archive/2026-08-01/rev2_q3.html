Read this honestly

The rung is directionally supported and magnitudinally unproven, and those are different verdicts. Supported: wide beats tight on 95% of bootstrap resamples, there is a real parameter plateau (2.5R / 3.0R / 3.5R + wide all land in +$72–84, so the answer is not a knife-edge), both time-halves agree in sign and roughly in size, and no single session carries it (LOO-worst is still +$34 to +$51). Unproven: strip the three best trades and +$62 becomes +$10. Eighty-four percent of the advantage lives in 3 of 24 entries. That is not a reason to disbelieve it — a ride exit is supposed to make its money in the top decile, and the median R-MFE of 4.26 with a p90 of 11.8 says the tail is genuinely there. It is a reason to refuse to call it proven at n=24, which is exactly what the sweep's own robustness filter did when it returned best = None for this cell.

The pre-registered PASS test — written now, so it cannot be moved later

The RIDE rung is declared PROVEN for grind_long | BIG-TREND only if all six hold on the graded sample:

1. n ≥ 39 paired BIG-TREND entries (derived: paired sd $247 ÷ diff $79.4, 2σ).  2. mean(RIDE − tight scalp) > 0 with bootstrap P > 0.95.  3. strip-best-3 paired diff > 0 — currently +$10.1, the weakest link and the one most likely to fail.  4. leave-one-session-out worst paired diff ≥ 0.  5. sign agreement across both time-halves (pre-07-16 / post-07-16) and across the 5s tier and the 250 ms tier.  6. the headline number is measured at 250 ms, with the 5s number reported alongside as the optimism gap.

On FAIL: grind_long drops to the proven MED-TREND exit and the RIDE rung is retired as a myth — note this is a real change, not a no-op, because the wide default is live today. On still-short at the backstop: same action. The asymmetry from [[router-benching-lessons]] applies to exits too — a wrongly-tight exit costs missed tail; a wrongly-wide exit costs give-back on every trade that never runs, and 62% of these entries lose money even with the wide exit (WIDEx2 win rate on the cell: 38%).

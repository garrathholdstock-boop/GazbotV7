M3 The greenfield lab — the gold book-break hunt, and the four hunts that never ran

★ READ THIS FIRST — what is here and what is not

This movement was scheduled as five separate hunts: one per MNQ cause-cluster (VACUUM, FLOW-LED, OPEN-NEWS), a chop-day scalp lab, and a dedicated gold hunt. Four of the five never started. The serial runner ran out of budget after the rehabilitation phase overran — BUDGET: stopping section builds to protect the tail (0m left) — and the synthesis phase that reads all five never ran either. So there is no MNQ greenfield hunt in this report. That is a gap, not a null: nobody looked, so nobody found nothing. It is named in the disposition table and it is the first thing to reschedule.

The gold hunt did get far enough to save its signal file before it died, and that file is a real dataset: 397 MGC book breaks across 22 sessions from 16 July, each with the order-book features it was built to test and the forward outcome. It is scored below to the full discipline — placebo, strip-the-best-three, leave-one-day-out, the size escalation, the excursion statistic and the book-separation table. Additional gold work that DID complete is in Part 2.5 Part A (the MGC day rider, the coil bouncer, the London-open break fade) — that is where the two gold SHADOW candidates of the week live. This section is the fourth gold idea, and it is the one that failed.

The idea was a good one and it is the natural thing to try with L2 on a new instrument. Gold breaks a level; look at the order book at the moment it breaks; if the far side is empty — nothing resting in the way — the move should run, and if the far side is stacked it should stall. That is the far-side-depletion hypothesis, it is exactly what Movement 1 asks of the book on MNQ, and on 397 gold breaks it is not just absent, it is backwards. The raw signal makes −$291.00 at −$0.73 a go. The book features it was built on select the LOSERS. And the one feature that does separate — resting book imbalance — only works in a middle band and evaporates the moment you narrow to the biggest moves, which is the opposite of what the escalation was supposed to reveal.

★ THE FINDING — an empty book ahead of a gold break is a WARNING, not a green light

Read the bottom of that table, not the top. “Far side is empty” loses −$2,059.50 over 192 signals and sits at the 4.8th percentile of its placebo. “Far side thin” is the 3.7th. “Empty far side AND a clean trend” is the 3.6th and loses −$27.91 a go. Being in the 4th percentile is not noise — it means that of 2,000 random ways to pick 192 of these breaks, 1,904 of them did BETTER. The feature carries real information and the sign is the other way round: on gold, a level that breaks into a vacuum is a level nobody is defending because nobody is there, and price comes straight back. That is a refutation of the gate as specified, and simultaneously the most promising thing in this section — it is a fade lead, not a break lead, and it is PARKED as one.

3 · The one filter that separates — and why it is still not a gate

Resting book imbalance is the exception. Skew the book our way at the moment of the break and the population turns from −$291 to +$1,795 over 134 signals, at the 96th percentile of its placebo, still +$590 after stripping its best three and still +$970 after dropping its best day. That is the only row in the study that survives all four checks. Then you cut it finer and it falls over:

5 · The excursion statistic — why no stop can save this

what was measuredvaluewhat it means
median favourable excursion3.28×ATRthe typical break does eventually go our way by about three ATR
median adverse excursion-3.04×ATR… and goes against us by almost exactly the same amount first
signals reaching 2×ATR our way66.0%two-thirds do offer a real move at some point
signals going 1×ATR against us first80.4%four-fifths hurt before they help — this is the number that kills it
signals that ‘ran’48.4%a coin flip, by the detector's own definition

Median MFE 3.28 ATR against median MAE −3.04 ATR is a symmetric distribution. There is no asymmetry to build a stop around: any stop tight enough to make the losers small takes out 80% of the population before it works, and any stop wide enough to survive costs more than the winners pay. This single table is why the exit rehabilitation that rescued exhaustion_short in Part 1.5 cannot rescue this — there, the entry beat 60 of 60 random controls; here, the entry is the coin flip.

M1 The biggest runs on MNQ this week — did we show up?

Forget what the desk did this week — start from the raw tape. MNQ printed 68 runs of 1.5×ATR or bigger in the last seven days (a 15-minute move of at least 44 points, against a typical 15-min range of 29). That is the whole tape, not a top-ten. We caught 3, we were positioned against 5, and we sat out 60. The runs we aligned with banked +$237 of honest money — but that is only 50% of their $470 hindsight ceiling, and the 60 we sat out left $8024 on the table. ★ REV2 — the bleed is NOT the fading. On this week’s census the five runs we were positioned against came to +$91.50, not a loss — and even that is generous, because +$434.00 of it is the DAY RIDER, a different desk that happened to be short inside one of the windows. Strip the rider out and the tournament’s fading around the runs is −$342.50 on 5 runs, which is real but small. The money on the table is the 60 runs we were not in at all and their $8,024 ceiling. This is a not-showing-up problem, not a fading problem, and that is what Movements 2 and 3 go after.

T1 — PROFIT-TAKING TRIAL (technique 1 of the ladder).  Declared 2026-10-09 BEFORE any model call.
Operator brief: "a trial on one day or 2 days ... see if Claude can maximise profit takes ... the only thing he has to
focus on. Hone each technique one by one. Maximum 8 trades. Let's give him great entries ... enter when I do, 50% into
a MIL ... he needs to manage the exit ... telegram at $200 and every $50 above ... maybe from $150, then he watches
every minute."   (Not an S-line iteration: no rule text, no gate, no champion change. It is a lab on ONE skill.)

WHAT IS TESTED
  Exit skill only. Can Claude, watching a trade the way the operator does, leave NEAR THE TOP and IN PROFIT, and does it
  beat plain mechanical exits on the identical entries?

ENTRIES (not Claude's) - DISCLOSED AS A HINDSIGHT LAB CONSTRUCT
  Every major intraday leg (MIL = the repo tracker: ends on a 7xATR give-back, MIL_K=7) of >= 60 pt whose 50% point
  falls inside 02:00-13:30Z. Entry signal = close of the first 1-min bar that has covered 50% of the leg's FINAL move;
  fill = close of the NEXT bar (+half spread), 4 lots, $8/pt, fee $1.50/lot RT. The leg total is known only because
  the day is finished. So: (a) these entries are not tradeable, (b) every one is guaranteed >= half a leg of room, so
  the trade is shielded from the usual never-green loss, (c) the result measures EXIT skill and must never be quoted
  as a strategy P&L. Max 8 trades/day is a LAB constraint (Law 0e forbids a cap in the real line); it does not bind
  here (4 and 2 entries). A new entry that arrives while a trade is open closes it first (FLIP_NEXT_ENTRY) - that is
  how a never-exit looks, and it is scored.

THE WATCH MECHANIC (the operator's own)
  No model call until open profit first reaches +$150 (18.75 pt) on a completed 1-min close. Alerts at $150 and every
  +$50 above (logged on the page). From the first alert Claude is called at EVERY completed minute until the trade is
  closed. Answers: HOLD or EXIT. No stop, no target, no trailing rule is given: the model is the exit. A trade that
  never reaches $150 is not watched and rides to the next entry or 13:30Z (scored as never_alerted).
  Brief: T1_BRIEF in scripts/t1_profit_take.py, sha 3d08960b1af72210. Page ~660 tokens: position, open/best/give-back
  ($, pt, ATR), alerts, path since entry (5-min), last 15 min (1-min), last 4 h (15-min), minutes to flat, its own last
  4 looks. The page carries NO leg end, no MIL info and no 7xATR statement (S5 showed the model obeys a quoted
  tolerance over the exit-in-profit instruction).
  Tools off, model claude-sonnet-5, fresh call per look. Harness: scripts/t1_profit_take.py sha 1985874d350e032f.

TIMING / CAUSALITY (look-ahead flag from the S6 audit - VERIFIED)
  Lake 1-min bar_ts is the minute START and its close is the price 55 s later (checked against 5 s bars on 3 days:
  120/120 start-labelled, <=9/120 end-labelled). sim_week_recursive.run_day keeps bars with bar_ts <= now, so a look
  labelled t actually sees, and fills at, the close of the minute starting at t: an off-by-one LABEL with a
  ZERO-LATENCY fill. Not a peek at information beyond the fill price, and identical in every S arm, so it does not
  bias arm-vs-arm comparisons; it does make absolute $ optimistic. (My first "honest re-mark" of the S arms was
  invalid - it filled at prices the model never saw - and is withdrawn.) T1 therefore uses: a bar is complete only when
  bar_ts+60 <= now; decision at that close; fill at the NEXT bar's close. Same rule for Claude and every baseline.
  The S-line records are not edited.

PRE-REGISTERED SCORE (all reported whatever the outcome; n=6 trades is a TRIAL, not a result - Law L6)
  M1 share of the leg remainder captured (sum exit pt / sum available pt to the leg top)   [B2 0.406, oracle 0.908]
  M1b share of the trade's own best captured (sum exit pt / sum best-close pt; can exceed 1 when it exits on a spike)
  M2 alerted trades closed in profit (pnl_usd > 0)                                          [controls 6/6]
  M3 mean give-back from the best (pt)         M4 alerted trades that ended red             M5 total $ net of fees
  PASS needs all of: M2 >= 5/6, M5 > $2,220 (beat the best control), M1 >= 0.45. Anything less: PARTIAL if it beat
  B0/B1 only, else FAIL. A PASS earns a wider run (more days, same entries) - never a ship.
  A day with > 10% errored calls is poisoned (L7) and is re-run, not scored. Max 900 calls/day.

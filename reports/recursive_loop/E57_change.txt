E57 (2026-10-12) PROFIT LOCK ON THE ONE-LOT CELLS. Declared BEFORE code runs. One rule family: a lock-in-profit exit. Law 0e: it NEVER exits at a loss and is not a stop-loss; it only turns a trade that was already in profit into a small win instead of letting it drift back and sit in the stuck tail.
DATA STATUS, SAID UP FRONT: TUNE (NQ 2021-22) in-sample. CONFIRM-A/B and SEALED were all seen by E52 and E56: second, third looks. No clean block exists. Pass = PROMISING at best.
WHY: 8-12% of 1-lot trades never reach +a and end at 20:40Z at about -$400. Many of those were up before they failed (mean favourable excursion not yet measured; E57 reports it first, as a count).
1. RULE. Once the trade's favourable excursion reaches m points (a bar high/low, +0.25 trade-through), arm a lock at entry+k. From the NEXT bar on, if the bar trades back to entry+k, exit there with 0.25pt adverse slippage (fill at k-0.25). k >= 2.5 so the net is positive after $1.50 fee. If the target a is reached before the lock triggers, take the target. Same-bar conflict is resolved against us (lock first). Otherwise hard flat 20:40Z as before. No stop below entry. K=1 flat-only on the EXIT time.
2. CELLS (from E56 frozen list, nothing re-selected): model theta0.7 CUT18 a=25 and a=30; model theta0.7 CUT19:30 a=30; control c0.35 CUT18 a=15 and 12.5. Grid on TUNE only: m in {7.5,10,12.5,15,20} (m<a), k in {2.5,4,6,8} (k<m). 
3. FIRST REPORT (counts): of the baseline stuck trades, the share that had reached +m before 20:40Z, for each m.
4. SELECTION. Champion = the same cell with NO lock. A lock cell must pass bible.gate vs it on TUNE. Freeze the best 3 (R) lock settings over the 5 cells, plus the best control. Then CONFIRM-A, CONFIRM-B, SEALED, with FLIP and RANDOM (random draws use the same lock rule).
5. VERDICT. PASS = $/day positive on all three later blocks AND passes bible.gate vs the no-lock cell on at least two. PROMISING = positive on two of three, beats FLIP on those. Else REFUTED. Ranked list either way.

RESULT A (TUNE only, in-sample; written before CONFIRM-A/B or SEALED are run)
COUNTS FIRST: of the model's stuck trades (theta0.7 CUT18, a=30; n=122 of 1,224), 68% had reached +7.5pt, 57% +12.5pt, 33% +20pt before failing. So most of the tail was in profit first. Control (c0.35, a=15): 43% reached +7.5, 26% +10.
Lock effect, model CUT18 a=30 (no lock +$18.6, R .070, stuck 9.7%, SD 265): best-R lock m7.5/k6 +$15.4, R .089, stuck 2.9%, SD 173, 399/508 days+. m12.5/k8 +$17.3, R .087, SD 198.

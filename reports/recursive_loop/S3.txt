1. CLAIM THE PROFIT WHILE IT IS GREEN, SIZED TO THE TAPE. When YOUR POSITION shows an open profit of about two times the ATR(14,1m) printed on the page or more, take it: exit at that look. Because the target is a multiple of ATR it scales by itself: in a quiet, low-ATR grind a dozen points is a full claim; in a fast, high-ATR market the same dozen points is noise and the target is correspondingly far away, so let the points accumulate until you reach it. Do not wait for the leg to stall, tire or reverse first; by the time it does, the profit is usually gone and the exit lands in the red. A claimed profit is a good trade and the leg is not lost: if the tape keeps grinding the same way, you may join it again on the usual entry judgement. This is not a stop and not a cap on anything. It says nothing about positions that are red or below the target, and it says nothing about entries.

TWO CHECKS THAT CAN ONLY STOP AN ENTRY. They are never a reason to enter: when neither applies, decide exactly as you would without them, and do not mention them.
1. EXTREME BURST ONLY. If the 15-minute bar that just made the new extreme has a high-low range at least FOUR TIMES the typical 15-minute bar of the previous three hours, do not enter on it. Wait at least 30 minutes, then decide afresh. A strong bar, a breakout bar, or a bar up to about three times normal is NOT this - join those exactly as you normally would.
2. NO NEW ENTRY AFTER 12:45Z. After 12:45Z you may hold or exit, never enter.

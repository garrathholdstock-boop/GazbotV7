FEATURES (causal: bars closed before the slot; computed by a new script from the same bars and the same detector run; fixed list):
  G1 since_ext_min  = minutes since the bar that set the leg's running CLOSE extreme.                 Bands 0-15, 15-30, 30-60, 60-120, 120-240, 240+.
  G2 pullback_atr   = (leg close extreme - entry, in the leg direction) / ATR14 at entry.             Bands <2, 2-4, 4-6, 6-8, 8-11, 11+  (11 = the confirm threshold K).
  G3 retest_gap_atr = (leg HIGH extreme (long) - highest high of the last 30 min) / ATR14; mirror for shorts: how far the last 30 min failed to get back to the extreme. Bands <0.5, 0.5-2, 2-4, 4+.
  G4 new_ext_rate   = share of the last 12 closed 5-min blocks whose close made a new leg close extreme (momentum still printing). Bands 0, (0-.25], (.25-.5], >.5.

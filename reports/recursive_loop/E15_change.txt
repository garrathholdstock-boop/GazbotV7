E15 - THE LEG ROBOT: DETECTOR v1 + PROFIT-TAKE LADDER, MECHANICAL, LIVE ON PAPER FROM 2026-10-11T22:00Z (declared 2026-10-09 before building; Law 3)
Operator, 2026-10-09: "I'm pretty satisfied. It looks like edge to me. Can you turn this into a build to run from Sunday night midnight open?"
This is a DEPLOYMENT of the frozen E6/E7/E14 rules, not a new rule. Nothing in the detector, the entry window, the sizing or the exit tiers is changed. It is the first FORWARD test of them: E9-E14 are all backtests on days that neighbour or precede the fit.

WHAT IT IS  scripts/leg_robot.py, a systemd oneshot every minute at :20. It builds the same 1-min series the sim builds, runs the same causal zigzag (K=11 x ATR14), and when a turn was confirmed in the last 150s it PRESSES THE RIDER'S OWN BUY/SELL REQUEST FILE (data/day_rider_buy.txt) with a per-lot dollar target list. It never touches the broker. The rider (src/gazbot7/day_rider.py) places the order exactly as it does for the operator's button: venue_first_ok, ownership, booking, ladder exits, 20:40Z hard flat all stay inside the rider. No new order path exists.
RULES (FROZEN, same as E6/E7/E14): detector v1 K=11, ATR14, causal zigzag on 1-min closes, series = 4 prior calendar session-days + the session day (session day = 22:00Z prev day .. 21:00Z). Decision at confirm bar close; BUY on a turn up, SELL on a turn down. Entries only when FLAT (a turn while holding is skipped, logged). Entries to 19:40Z. Hard flat 20:40Z by the rider. 4 lots. NO stop, NO killer, NO flip, NO chop guard, NO trail.
ARM  = H, per-lot targets $25/$50/$75/$100 = 12.5/25/37.5/50 pt (file field 25,50,75,100). WHY H AND NOT F: F made more (40d: F $28,238 vs H $20,109) but bible.gate (Law 0) needs days-positive, worst day and spread/mean not to worsen and F's worst day is -$2,398 vs H -$2,024 on the same 40 days, 32/40 positive for both. Consistency outranks return, so H. F is a one-line change (ARM constant) and is a NEW declared change.
SWITCH = data/leg_robot.env  leg_robot=on|off  (missing or anything else = off). start_after 2026-10-11T22:00:00Z and stop_after 2026-10-30T21:00:00Z are constants in the script, so the timer can be enabled now and does nothing until Sunday.
WHY stop_after 2026-10-30: (1) US DST ends 2026-11-01; the CME open moves to 23:00Z and the 22:00Z session anchor, 19:40Z entry end and 20:40Z floor stop meaning what the rules froze. (2) capture.db.bars has no contract column; the Dec roll would splice about 297pt of basis into the series unmarked. Extending past either needs a declared change.

GATES, in order (every refusal is logged with its reason; none is retried; a signal is handled once, keyed by its confirm timestamp):
 1 switch on, inside start/stop · 2 the newest completed bar is no older than 180s (a dead feed is not a quiet tape) · 3 signal age <= 150s at first sight (older = STALE, logged, not entered) · 4 decision time <= 19:40Z · 5 data/day_rider.env day_rider=on · 6 rider heartbeat <= 120s old, not entered, lots_open 0, venue_ok true, venue_net 0, not blind_holding · 7 no unread request already in the file (never overwrite the operator's press) · 8 detector source hash matches the frozen one, else REFUSE (a silent edit to the detector is not a deployment of E6).
FLAT-ONLY also means the operator can trade the same account by hand: while he holds, the robot is silent; a turn that confirms during his trade is skipped like any turn-while-holding.

KNOWN LIMITS, STATED BEFORE THE FIRST SIGNAL
 - Backtest fills are optimistic (next-bar close +/- 0.125). Paper multi-lot fills are fabricated adverse at exactly 0.1% on lots 2-4 (CLAUDE.md trap 13), so paper P&L will UNDERSTATE a clean fill on multi-lot exits and entries; a fill-cost haircut run of the 40 days is still owed and is NOT done.
 - K=11 and the 50pt take were chosen on days neighbouring the 113 entries; the lake weeks are out-of-sample for that choice but not for the idea. This is the first genuinely forward evidence.
 - Losses are concentrated in a few no-stop reversal trades (40d: 9 red entries H, worst -$2,598; worst day -$2,024). 4 lots with no stop is $246k notional on paper. There is no rule for a deep reversal; the 20:40Z flat is the only floor. That is the operator's standing choice (no stops) and is not changed here.
 - Every robot press is a write of day_rider_buy.txt, which gazbot7-capture-read-buy.path snapshots into data/operator_reads.jsonl as if the operator had pressed. Robot presses are identifiable there by the targets string and are listed in data/leg_robot.jsonl; they must be EXCLUDED from any operator-read fitting (the 30-press floor counts his presses, not the robot's). Existing scripts are not modified.
 - The rider sends a critical Telegram on every manual-entry fill (about 3 per day expected from the 40-day entry rate of 2.8/day).
 - The robot reads capture.db 5s bars. A partial or gappy final minute is possible in thin tape; only fully elapsed minutes are used.
 - FORWARD READ-OUT: counts first (signals, entered, skipped-holding, stale, refused-by-gate), then dollars from the rider's booked trades only, never an interim. Compare against the same signals replayed through the sim on the same days. First honest read after 10 session days (about 2026-10-23). INTERIM, NOT A RESULT.

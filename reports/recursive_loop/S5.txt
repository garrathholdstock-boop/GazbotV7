1. HOLD THROUGH THE PULLBACKS AND LEAVE IN PROFIT. This replaces the brief's "the first honest sign is enough" and "a decent profit, about 25-40 points, is a good trade": a leg on this tape runs about 250-300 points, and the pullbacks inside it are about 80 points (about 7 times the ATR(14,1m) printed on the page; use the ATR multiple, not 80 points, when the tape is quiet or fast). A pullback of less than about 7 ATR from the best price your trade has reached is the leg breathing, not the leg ending: stay in, and say how many ATR the pullback is. Leave when the pullback from the best price is bigger than about 7 ATR AND the DAY VIEW shows the move you joined has turned, not just one bar: take it at that look, in profit. You must leave in profit as much as possible. A trade that has been well green (2 ATR or more) is not allowed to finish red: once the day-scale move has turned, take what is left while it is still green, and do not wait for it to come back. A trade that is red because you joined early stays while the leg you joined is intact and leaves only when that leg has been undone, as in the brief; do not leave a red trade just because of a pullback. This is not a stop and not a cap on anything, and it says nothing about entries.

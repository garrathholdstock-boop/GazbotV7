TWO CHECKS BEFORE ANY ENTRY (they override "joining late is fine"):
1. DO NOT BUY THE CLIMAX. Look at the last 15-30 minutes. If price has just burst to a new extreme in a move that is clearly the biggest, fastest push of the session so far - a 15-minute bar far larger than the bars before it, a "sharp break", a "strong push" out of a quiet base - do NOT enter on that burst. On this desk that burst at the end of a long leg is where the worst losses came from: the extreme it prints is usually the end of the leg, and with no stop the position then sits hundreds of points offside until the 20:40Z flat. WAIT instead. Enter only if a pullback follows, holds above the base the burst came from (below it, for a short), and the leg then turns back in its direction.
2. NO NEW ENTRY AFTER 12:45Z. A position opened in the last 45 minutes of the window must ride the US cash open with no time left to be right. After 12:45Z you may hold or exit, never enter.
Otherwise trade exactly as before: with the leg, and joining late is fine while the leg is still grinding in normal-sized steps.

1. A CLAIM IS A PROFIT WORTH HAVING, AND THE PAGE TELLS YOU WHICH TRADES WERE ONE. A claim is a trade that closed green by at least 2 times the ATR(14,1m) AND at least $200. The harness decides it and prints it on the scorecard ("CLAIMED" or "NOT a claim"). When YOUR POSITION shows an open profit at that level, take it: exit at that look. Do not wait for the leg to stall, tire or reverse first. This applies at that level and above it only (see rule 6 for a green trade below it). A smaller green exit is fine, but it is not a claim and it does not entitle you to come back in. This is not a stop and not a cap on anything.
2. ONE TRADE PER MAJOR MOVE, UNLESS THE LAST ONE WAS A CLAIM. A new entry needs either (a) a major move (the numbered MIL on the scorecard) with no trade of yours in it yet, or (b) a claim followed by the rejoin test in rule 3. After a trade that was not a claim, do not enter again in the same major move; wait for the next one. Your first trade of the day is always allowed. This is a condition on the next entry only. It never asks you to close or leave a trade you hold, and it says nothing about stopping for the day.
3. REJOIN A GRIND ONLY AFTER A REAL CLAIM, AND ONLY WHEN IT IS STILL GOING. If your last trade was a claim, you may rejoin the same direction only when at least 15 minutes (three looks) have passed since it closed AND price is still beyond the best price that claimed trade reached. The scorecard prints both facts. Not the very next look. If you did not claim a decent profit, you do not jump back in. This replaces any earlier wording about joining again on the usual entry judgement.
4. AFTER THE SIXTH TRADE OF THE DAY, ONLY ONE REASON COUNTS. From your seventh trade of the day onward, the only valid reason to enter is: my last trade was a claim and the move is still running (the rejoin test in rule 3 passes). Write that reason; if you cannot, WAIT. This is not a limit on how many trades a day can have. A grind can carry on, one claim and one rejoin at a time.
7. START EVERY REASON WITH THE DAY, THEN THE BOOK, THEN NOW. Begin your reason with `DAY:` what today has done so far, then `BOOK:` your trade count, your last claim and the trades in this major move, then `NOW:` what the close-in view shows. Write it before you decide.

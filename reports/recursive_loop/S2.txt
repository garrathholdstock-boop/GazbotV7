1. NEVER ENTER AGAINST THE MIL. When you are FLAT and thinking of an entry, read the "MAJOR INTRADAY LEG (MIL)" line on the page. If the MIL is UP you may only go LONG; if it is DOWN you may only go SHORT. The page prints ENTER_LONG / ENTER_SHORT as ALLOWED or NOT ALLOWED - a NOT ALLOWED entry is not made, however good the last few bars look. A bounce or a dip against the MIL is the MIL still running, not a new leg; the MIL ends only when price gives back 7x ATR from its extreme, and the page will tell you when it has. If neither side is allowed, stay flat and wait.
2. ONE ENTRY PER MIL AND SIDE, UNLESS THE MIL IS OLD. Once you have entered a side inside the current MIL, do not enter that same side again until the MIL is 176 minutes old. The page prints your entries in this MIL and whether the second is allowed yet. This is not a cap on trades: a new MIL gives a fresh entry, and an old, drawn-out MIL (176 min or more) allows you to keep re-entering and harvesting with it.
These two rules govern ENTRIES ONLY, and only when you are flat. They say nothing about exits: how you hold, when you take a profit and when you get out are exactly as in the brief.

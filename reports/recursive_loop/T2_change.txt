QUESTION   After Claude has banked a profit and left a leg early, can it get back in the SAME direction and take more of
           the same leg, net of the extra spread and fees, without turning a banked profit into a loss?

DESIGN = A PAIRED INCREMENT ON T1  (scripts/t2_reentry.py sha 69cea0373ca0d503; T1 harness unchanged)
  * Same days (2026-03-05, 2026-04-30), same hindsight lab entries (50% bar of each >=60 pt MIL), same costs, same timing
    (decide at a completed bar's close, fill at the NEXT bar's close), same $150/+$50 alerts.
  * The FIRST trade of every leg REPLAYS Claude's own T1 exit decisions (read from T1_<day>_claude.json), so the leg
    exits are identical to T1 and the ONLY new thing is what happens after them. Control C0 reproduces T1 exactly
    (1,374 + 848 = $2,222) — verified before any call.
  * After an exit the lab watches for 60 min. Each completed minute Claude is asked WAIT|REENTER (same direction only).
    The page: exit price/time, banked $, high of the move, pullback depth (pt and ATR), pullback now, tape since exit
    (20 x 1-min, 4 h x 15-min), watch time left, buy-backs left. It does NOT carry the leg end, the leg size or any MIL
    figure. A re-entry fills at the next bar's close. A re-entered trade is watched EVERY minute from its entry (no $150
    gate, he would be watching), same exit brief as T1 plus a one-line note it is a buy-back. No stop, no target.
  * Cap (LAB constraint; Law 0e forbids a cap in the real line): 8 trades/day, with the lab entries reserved first, so
    re-entries allowed = 8 - lab entries: 4 on 03-05, 6 on 04-30. The next lab entry cancels any watch and flips as in T1.
  * Brief shas: re-entry brief 0f0aa1a0382d8cbf, exit brief (T1 brief + buy-back note) 911619a661462d9d; script sha above.
  * Hindsight caveat as T1: entries are not tradeable and every leg has guaranteed room; the re-entry question is only
    "does it resume", but a re-entry on a leg that has actually finished is possible (that is the failure measured).

NOTHING IN THIS STUDY USES ANY PARAMETER, RULE OR NUMBER OF THE LIVE ROBOT. Not compared against it either. Controls are generic (below). Robot/rider/account untouched. No commit.

1. WHAT A MIL IS (hindsight, detector-free). S = mean session range (high-low) of the previous 20 sessions. T = 0.40*S. A MIL top (bottom) is a pivot of the hindsight zigzag with reversal T on 1-min highs/lows: a bar whose high (low) is the extreme of a leg that was then reversed by >= T. T ~ 150pt at 2025 volatility. Sessions = UTC date D, bars [D-2h, D+21h). Hindsight window ends 20:40Z.
2. THE DETECTOR (causal, price extremes). At every minute t in the entry window (06:00Z <= t < 19:30Z) and for each side, the candidate pivot is the running extreme (highest high / lowest low) over the trailing window; up to 4 trailing windows (45/90/180/360 min) -> distinct pivots. It FIRES when a learned score for "this extreme is a MIL pivot" reaches threshold theta, the first time per pivot. Score = LightGBM on causal features only: retrace from the extreme (closes and lows/highs) in S and in ATR14 units, minutes since the extreme, retrace speed, 5/15/30-min momentum, run-in to the extreme, left-dominance of the extreme, bounce/lower-high structure since the trough, volume ratios (retrace vs run-in, pivot-bar spike), minute of day, range-so-far position. Bars only (no ticks). Label = "is a hindsight zigzag pivot" (primary). No feature touches the future.
   STATE: alternate. A new fire needs its pivot to be LATER than the previous fire's pivot (no stale extremes); same-side re-fire only if the new extreme is beyond the old one (the old call failed).
3. CONTROL (generic, not the robot): the same candidate pivots; fire when the close retraces >= c*S from the extreme. c swept 0.08..0.50. Same state rule. This is "plain retrace from the extreme", the thing any trader would draw.
4. DETECTOR TEST (frozen). On TUNE then CONFIRM, for each model theta and each control c compute: fires/day, primary precision, recall of hindsight pivots, median lag (min and points) of true fires. VERDICT "BETTER": at matched precision 0.50/0.60/0.70 (control interpolated on its own lag-vs-precision curve) the model's median lag is <= 80% of the control's at >= 2 of the 3 levels, with recall not more than 0.05 lower, on TUNE AND on CONFIRM. Otherwise the model is no better than plain retrace and that is the result.
5. THE TRADE (declared, 2 LOTS, MNQ-equivalent $2/pt/lot, fee $1.50 RT per lot, entry at the fire-bar close +0.25pt adverse, limit exits need a trade-through of 0.25pt). On a fire, if FLAT, enter 2 lots in the new leg's direction. Lot A takes profit at +a, lot B at +b. NO STOP (Law 0e). Hard flat at 20:40Z (bar close). Flat-only: a fire while any lot is open is skipped (counted). Grid a in {5,7.5,10,12.5,15,17.5,20,25}, b in {20,25,30,35,40,50,60,80,100,150} (b>a). 12.5/35 is the operator's example and is ALWAYS reported.
   Older years are expressed in TODAY'S POINTS: prices scaled by S_ref/S_day (S_ref = mean S over NQ 2025-01-30..07-24). SEALED is reported both ways.
6. CONTROLS FOR THE CALL ITSELF: FLIP (same fire times, opposite direction) and RANDOM (same number of fires per day, random minutes in the window, random side; 30 draws; report mean and 95th percentile). A detector whose P&L does not beat its own flip is not calling anything.
7. SELECTION (frozen). Rank key R = ($/day) / (daily SD). Need >= 0.5 trades/day. Champion = the best CONTROL cell on TUNE (its own best c and best (a,b)) by R. A challenger cell must pass bible.gate vs that champion on TUNE; shortlist = top 3 by R, FROZEN with (theta,a,b) before CONFIRM is opened. Then CONFIRM (frozen cells, gate vs the frozen champion). Then SEALED once, the frozen shortlist plus the 12.5/35 reference.
8. BLOCKS. TRAIN NQ 2015-01..2020-12 (model fit; last 12 months early-stop only). TUNE NQ 2021-01..2022-12 (theta, c, a, b chosen here). CONFIRM-A NQ 2023-01..2024-08-11 (never scored by any earlier study of mine). CONFIRM-B NQ 2024-08-12..2025-07-24 (earlier studies scored OTHER detectors on it; not a fit set for this one; labelled). SEALED = MNQ backfill 2025-09-15..2026-03-08 (before the 2026-03-09 POOL that earlier work saw; never opened by anything). Opened ONCE, at the end.
9. IN-SAMPLE STATEMENT (said up front): TRAIN and TUNE are in-sample for the model / the grid. CONFIRM is out-of-sample for the frozen cells but the T=0.4S, entry window and claim-grid choices were set before looking and by my reading of earlier studies of this tape (not independent of them). SEALED is the only fully clean block and it is a different instrument/contract set.
10. TUNING CAP. Detector: one training run; ONE declared revision allowed (features/params) only if section 4 fails on TUNE, logged in a dated appendix before it is run. Claims: one grid pass on TUNE. No other tuning. Every study ends in a RANKED SHORTLIST.

1. PATIENCE WHEN RED: A SUDDEN MOVE IS NOT PROOF. If you hold a position that is RED and the newest 15-minute bar on the page (the last 15 minutes, with the right-hand edge of the shape chart) shows a sharp move against it, do not exit on that look, even if it looks as though the leg has reversed: a sharp move in one or two bars is the move that most often snaps back, and cutting at the worst bar locks the loss in. Give the move until it has aged out of the newest bar, about fifteen minutes: if the position is still red and still moving against you by then, treat it as real and use the exit judgement in the brief as normal. This is not a stop and not a cap on anything. It never applies to a profit: a profit that is stalling or tiring is taken exactly as before. It says nothing about entries. On such a look this rule takes precedence over the brief's instruction to get out when the leg has reversed; on every other look the brief applies unchanged.

=== THE LIQUIDITY-PROVISION ARITHMETIC (MNQ) ===
A perfect market maker buys the bid and sells the offer: it earns the FULL spread, once, per
round turn. On MNQ that is 1 tick = 0.25 pt = $0.50 gross.
This desk's commission is $1.50 per round trip per contract (CLAUDE.md, and the fills table's
own commission column). $0.50 gross - $1.50 commission = -$1.00 per round turn, BEFORE adverse
selection, BEFORE queue position, and BEFORE the fact that a retail order joins the back of a
queue that is hundreds of contracts deep.
=> Providing liquidity in MNQ at a retail commission schedule is arithmetically loss-making on
   every single fill. It is not a hard edge; it is a negative constant.

=== LIQUIDITY PROVISION, with the MEASURED spread ===
Median MNQ spread is 0.50 pt = 2 ticks = $1.00, not 1 tick (only 17.3%% of quotes are 1 tick wide).
A market maker capturing the FULL measured spread once per round turn earns $1.00 gross and pays
$1.50 commission: -$0.50 per round turn, before adverse selection and before queue priority.
Top of book is a MEDIAN OF 4 CONTRACTS on MNQ, so a retail resting order is behind a queue it
cannot see the front of. The idea is dead on arithmetic, not on difficulty.

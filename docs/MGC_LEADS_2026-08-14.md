## 4. ⚠⚠ THREE TRAPS THAT BIT TODAY — check for them in anything built tonight

1. **`resample` labels a bar by its LEFT edge.** The 10:00-10:01 bar is stamped `10:00`, but its
   close and range are only known at 10:01. Racing from `ts` replays the decision minute. **Cost: the
   coil gate fell +$906.55 → +$470.42.** Enter at `ts+60s` for a close-based signal; for a LEVEL
   entry, race from the tick that actually crosses it.
2. **`drift.DriftRead.ok` is NOT "fired".** `ok` means *"enough data to say anything at all"*; the
   field meaning the signal fired is **`confirmed`**. Gating on `ok` entered EVERY session and
   produced a fake "16/16 days fired, +12.5pp edge" on rows showing eff 0.01. `direction` is a
   **string** (`"UP"`/`"DOWN"`/`""`), not a sign.
3. **`lake.connect()` defaults to `symbol="MNQ"`.** For gold you must pass `lake.connect(symbol="MGC")`
   or you will silently query zero rows and report a confident NULL. MGC coverage: **18 bar-days /
   20 tick-days**, 07-07..07-17 (V5 archive) + 08-05..08-14, with a gap 07-18..08-04.

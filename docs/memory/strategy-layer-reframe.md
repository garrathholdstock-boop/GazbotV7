A strategy-layer review (operator pasted it 2026-06-20) reset the direction. Risk engine = "survived war";
strategy layer = "still discovering itself" = least-mature component, now gets ~all future effort. Operator
fully agreed; his framing: **"we are still chasing thresholds and not economic reasons"** and **"REGIME has to
be the first thing — it was a bull market, then CHOP, and we did this."** Recorded in `docs/GATE_CUBE_SCOPE.md`
top section (⚑ STRATEGY-LAYER REFRAME) — read that first before any grid/cube work.

**The trap I named (the review couldn't — it can't see our tooling):** the gate cube as scoped is a
threshold-chasing machine with a turbocharger; sweeping every threshold = *industrialised* overfitting, and the
courtroom would give false confidence because **it tests "is this real/not noise?" (FDR, deflated-Sharpe, OOS)
but NOT "is this economically coherent?"** A threshold can pass the stats and still be a coincidence + a story.

**Six principles we now build against:**
1. **REGIME IS THE SPINE — first, always.** Trending / Chop-Range / Shock / Dead. Every cycle stamped; every
   edge is a (regime × gate) CELL, never a gate alone. Signal→Trade becomes Regime→Signal→Trade.
2. **STOP ADDING GATES/THRESHOLDS** — we have enough; the gap is knowing *when* they work.
3. **MEASURE FORCE, not just position** — we bank *where* price is (vwap_dist) not *how hard* it moves. Add
   per-cycle: bar-return magnitudes (ATR units), leg sizes, acceleration, close-location-in-range, body/range +
   **cross-market/complex context** (MNQ when MES fires, breadth, leader/laggard). Cheap off existing bars, NO
   new feed. Highest-value capture add. (Ties [[analytics-capture-roadmap]], [[edge-spectrum-pipeline]].)
4. **FIND WHERE THE MONEY ALREADY COMES FROM = THE NEXT BUILD.** Label every winner+loser by
   regime/ATR/expansion/time/contract → where does profit concentrate (80/20)? Uses funnel + trades +
   edge_observations — buildable NOW, no new gate, before the cube. Two honest outcomes: restrict-to-paying-
   conditions OR (priors lean here) the entry MECHANISM is weak → rebuild, don't just filter.
5. **EVERY STRATEGY EXPLAINS ITSELF + courtroom tests the REASON.** Declare a one-line mechanism + the regime it
   should work in BEFORE testing; new verdict dimension = regime-concentration / economic coherence (edge must
   concentrate where the mechanism predicts; momentum-edge living in dead/chop = lying even if stats pass).
6. **LEAVE EXITS ALONE** — ratchet + flat-clock are the solved/mature half; PARK adaptive-ratchet-curve work;
   money now is entry quality. (Supersedes the exit-grid emphasis in [[chop-scalp-study]].)

**OPTIMISATION TARGET = expectancy after costs, NOT win-rate or trade-count (operator 2026-06-20, "just a dude
trying to earn a few bucks").** The operator's goal is modest, achievable, and small-size IS an advantage (we
live on scraps the big funds ignore). But "win more than we lose" by COUNT is a trap: mean-reversion's natural
shape is win-small-often / lose-big-rarely (fading an Information move that runs), so a high win-rate can hide
negative expectancy. What compounds = (avg win − avg loss − round-trip cost toll). The ratchet already makes
wins>losses (study: ratchet +$2,861); the leak is hundreds of no-edge entries bled out the flat-clock (−$4,082).
**The small player's real edge is PATIENCE/SELECTIVITY, not frequency** — sit out 95% of the rollercoaster, strike
only on the ~1-in-20 quiver where a pressure (immediacy/forced/exhaustion) is genuinely paying us; high trade-
count is the ENEMY of a thin edge (every trade pays the cost toll). **DESIGN RULE: never tune gates toward MORE
trades — tune toward fewer, higher-expectancy trades.** This is WHY pressure-labeling matters: it says when
someone's paying so we can skip the rest. (HFT market-makers eat the fast premium in microseconds; our only
space is the slow/big residual that overwhelms their inventory in thin liquidity — see the pressure/footprint
taxonomy in docs/MONDAY_CLEAN_CAPTURE_SCOPE.md.)

**Sequencing:** study-first (4) → force/cross-market/4-regime capture (3) → bend cube+courtroom to mechanism+
regime (1,5). Open operator steers: study-first vs lake-completeness-first (I lean study-first); canonicalise
the 4-regime taxonomy as-is vs operator defines regimes. Plain-English teaching for the operator (he's
self-described uneducated on the econ part): every trade must answer "who is forced to do what, and why will it
keep pushing price my way long enough to pay?" — numbers are evidence of that story, not the reason.

Operator rule (2026-07-21): the multi-slot paper tournament's **roster changes** — relegating the worst gates and promoting from the shadow feeder — happen **ONLY on Saturdays, after the Friday-night report + analysis**. Never ad-hoc, never intra-week, never off a single day's P&L.

The dashboard's **🔻 relegation flags** (TOURNEY scoreboard bottom-2) and the **shadow PROMOTION FEEDER** panel are **LIVE INDICATORS that inform the Saturday decision** — they are NOT triggers to act on mid-week. Same for a gate having a bad day (e.g. rgv_short's −$171) or a shadow variant surging.

**Why:** a promotion/relegation needs a full week of cross-regime evidence + the Friday analysis to judge; knee-jerk mid-week swaps churn the lineup (the re-entry-churn lesson, [[shadow-board-as-regime-router]]) and over-fit to noise. The weekly cadence pairs with the Friday report being the laboratory ([[friday-report-full-pipeline-guarantee]], [[three-pass-adversarial-friday]]) and the earned-not-reactive promotion ladder ([[promotion-ladder-sizing]]).

**How to apply:** never propose or make a tournament roster change except on a Saturday following that week's Friday report. Mid-week, surface the indicators (flags/candidates/day-type) as *data for the Saturday review*, and hold. The live desk keeps running its current 6-gate lineup until then.

**★ THE TENSE IS THE WHOLE RULE. The chart is what IS happening (present). The shadow board is what
HAS BEEN happening (past).** Direction comes from the present tense, never from the past one.

**⚠ The trap is not ignorance of this rule — it is a memory that contradicts it.**
[[shadow-board-as-regime-router]] is TITLED and DESCRIBED as "the shadow board IS the regime router",
and that is the superseded idea; its own bottom section retracts it (autocorr ≈0 momentum, −0.16 rgv,
"trading the equity curve"). On 08-06 I read the title, acted on it, and never reached the retraction.
Both files are now cross-linked and that file's description carries the warning. If a memory's title
and its conclusion disagree, the title is what gets acted on — fix the title.

**Why:** the shadow board records what has ALREADY worked this session. It is a lagging summary, so
leading with it means reading the regime that just ended and arriving one leg late — systematically.
Two errors on 08-06, both from the same mistake:

- **13:20Z** — endorsed arming `abs_veto_short` because every short variant was green
  (`thrust_short_raw` +246, `absveto55` +191, `sw_absS_*` +171) and every long variant red. The CHART
  at that moment showed a flush already ~250pt extended into a new low. The shadow was describing the
  leg that was ending. It fired at 29253, the extreme, and stopped both lots for **−$129.50**.
- **13:47–14:19Z** — declined three open-hour [ACT] calls to arm longs, partly on "the shadow says
  shorts". The chart showed eight consecutive higher highs. By the time `grind_fast` flipped
  −127 → +115 the move was **+297pt** old and the operator had to point out the desk was sitting out
  a 300-point climb he could see at a glance.

**How to apply:** form the read from PRICE FIRST — structure (higher highs/lows, break of a real
level, where price sits in the day's range), then ER/ATR on the SEGMENT, then extension vs VWAP.
State that read before opening the shadow board. Then use the shadow only to confirm or to veto —
never to generate the direction. A green shadow cell is a reason to look, never a reason to enter;
a red one does not forbid a trade the chart is clearly showing. Corollary already learned the hard
way the same day: judge the SEGMENT not the DAY aggregate — the day-ER/meter is diluted by earlier
chop and produced the same late-and-wrong answer ([[vol-expansion-is-the-binding-leg]],
[[intelligent-routing-not-blanket-benching]]). Related: [[shadow-sim-understates-losses]],
[[shadow-board-as-regime-router]] — that last one needs reading in light of this: the shadow board
is a REGIME CONFIRMER, not a regime detector.

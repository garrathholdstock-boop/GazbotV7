2026-08-01, verbatim: *"stop pinging me half baked findings. only final report."*

Said after I sent two Telegram notifications and several long in-chat updates while a multi-agent job was still running — one per agent as each landed, each one revising the previous conclusion.

**Why:** he is usually travelling or away from the desk when this work runs. Findings that are still being re-derived can reverse (on this very day, three of four headline recommendations were withdrawn or corrected *after* I had already messaged him about them). A ping that later needs retracting costs him attention and trust, and a stream of them is worse than silence.

**How to apply:** while long work is running, stay quiet. Do the work, verify it myself, and send ONE message when the deliverable is finished. Exception: a genuine blocker that only he can clear (e.g. a permission I need, or a live-risk item with a hard deadline) — that is worth one message, stated once, not repeated as it develops. Do not narrate agent-by-agent progress. Do not ping to say a revision is coming. See [[friday-findings-need-adversarial-rederivation]] for why interim findings are especially unsafe to relay.

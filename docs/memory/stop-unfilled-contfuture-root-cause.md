**The STOP_UNFILLED class root-caused (2026-07-27).** Symptom: ~18% of stop-exits since the multi-slot go-live book as `STOP_UNFILLED` (24 total, −$1,295; 4/4 overnight 07-26→27 ≈ −$200, all abs_veto — but abs_veto was just the only gate that hit a stop in the chop, NOT abs_veto-specific).

**Root cause (venue truth from the gazbot7-tournament journal):** every failed stop is a correctly-formed `StopLimitOrder` (our §352 fillable limit 20pt past trigger, right side — that fix is intact) but it sits `status=PreSubmitted, whyHeld='trigger'` from placement to cancel and **never fires the trigger** — even with price 10s+ past the level. It's NOT the limit gapping (that would show it triggered-then-unfilled); the TRIGGER never fires. Cause: the desk rests stops on the **continuous `ContFuture`** (`tournament.py` `qualifyContractsAsync(ContFuture(...))`), and IBKR intermittently won't fire a resting stop's trigger on a continuous contract. Entries are unaffected (marketable, execute instantly, no trigger). The `stop_breached` guard (multislot_core, STOP_BREACH_FLATTEN_STREAK=2 × PROTECT_INTERVAL_S=5s ≈ 10s) then market-flattens at ≈ the trigger → bounded loss ≈ intended stop + a few pts, exit_reason STOP_UNFILLED. So losses are bounded (not a runaway) but stops aren't working as designed; a fast gap during the ~10s guard window could bite worse. It's intermittent (109 clean STOP fills exist too, incl. 30 on 07-20) — partly a PAPER-account trigger-flakiness artifact; likely resolves on live, but resting on a continuous contract is the wrong setup regardless.

**Fix (operator: "contract-fix-first, build it on a branch"):** rest protective stops on the **concrete front-month `Future`** (qualified off the ContFuture's conId — same conId, so positions/naked-audit reconcile at the venue), entries/exits stay on the ContFuture. `IBBrokerAdapter` gained `stop_contract` (defaults to `contract`, back-compat); `place_stop` uses it; `tournament._build_live` qualifies the Future and passes it. +2 tests, full suite 445 green. Committed + pushed on branch `fix/stop-contract-concrete-future` (gazbot7 `99e3c11`).

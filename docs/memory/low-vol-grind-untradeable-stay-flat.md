**The rule:** not every real trend is tradeable. A **low-vol grind** has no profitable gate in the current set:
- **grind_long** self-gates OFF below its 0.35 ER floor (the desk-ER reads chop on a grind even as price climbs) — and when it does fire it churns.
- **abs_veto_long**'s 55s veto needs a *thrust* to pass; a slow driftless climb never gives one, or gives a fake micro-thrust that stops.
- The gates need **vol/thrust** (expanding ATR, a decisive push, real aggressor demand). A grind gives none — price ekes to marginal new highs then pulls back small, stopping any long that fired.

**How to apply:** when price is making new highs but ATR is compressed and the desk-ER is chop-low, **stay flat** — that is the *correct* call, not a missed opportunity. Do NOT force a re-arm just because price is at new highs (that's the [[rearm-momentum-needs-er-climb-not-delta-blip]] trap in a slower form). Re-arm only when a **vol-backed** break develops (ATR expanding + decisive push + real aggressor). The event watcher's REGIME→TREND/BREAK now require ATR≥18 for exactly this reason. Distinct from the fake-out lesson: this is about the *untradeability* of a genuine-but-quiet trend, not a fooled read.

**THE TAIL IS REAL — capping momentum at 2R is wrong:** reachable-MFE (peak before the native 1-ATR stop fires) shows **52% of momentum profit-dollars live BEYOND 2R** (25% of trades run ≥3R, max 27R). scalp-2R forfeits that half AND nets WORSE (−$2,368 vs current chandelier −$1,561 on momentum). Vindicates the operator.

**★ KEY COUNTERINTUITIVE FINDING — tightening `start_k` is a TRAP.** The obvious fix ("the 42pt give-back is too wide → tighten start_k") is exactly wrong: in EVERY ATR band a WIDER start_k (3.5) gives BOTH better tail-capture AND less whipsaw (tight 1.5 → 83-92% whipsaw, captures 9% of tail = pure range-scalping that shakes out of the runs). **The give-back on a wide trail is the PRICE of not whipsawing, not a bug.** The raw-net optimizer loves the tight config but it's a scalp — don't follow it in a range regime.

**THE FIX (levers are `tighten` + `min_k`, NOT `start_k`):** `tighten` 0.75→0.5 (don't yank mid-run), `min_k` 0.5→1.0 (lock a 1-ATR trail past ~5R so the deep tail stops leaking), and **DROP the vol-adaptive `chandelier_start_k` grade** — it TIGHTENS high-ATR entries, backwards (high-ATR needs the WIDE trail most). **Tuned config: `start_k=3.5, min_k=1.0, tighten=0.5`, flat (no grade).** Current chandelier captures only 23% of green-MFE (gives back 77%); tuned lifts whipsaw 42%→36% and on the biggest runs captures 40-140% more than current, ~2-2.5× a 2R cap.

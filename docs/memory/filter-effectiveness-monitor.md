**Why:** the ER/ATR floors are the desk's edge-selectivity layer; operator wants ongoing evidence they filter the bleed without over-clipping participation — a monitor, NOT a tuning trigger.

**How to apply:** `scripts/filter_check.py` (DuckDB-vectorized, the rule) reconstructs ER (30-min 1-min closes) + ATR (14-period 1-min bars) AT ENTRY — the gate's exact bases — for each trade, vs its ER_FLOOR/CEIL/BAND + ATR_FLOOR. Classifies each loser: **BORDERLINE** (hugs a threshold → a tighter floor catches it, at cost of participation), **SPIKE-ENTRY** (reconstructed ER below the floor → gate fired on an intra-minute live-tick spike the closed bar didn't confirm; violent-chop whipsaw — the floor's blind spot, not fixable by lifting the number), or **clean-in-regime** (well inside the window → the GATE lost, not a filter miss). `review(t0)`/`hour_summary(t0)` are imported by `scripts/hour_watch.py` → every hourly watch now prints a `FILTER(1h): …` line. CLI: `PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python scripts/filter_check.py --days N`.

Operator rule (07-30, live): **the US cash open (13:30 UTC / 15:30 Paris) is ALWAYS crazy — stop-runs and fake-outs are structural, not a regime signal.** Do NOT bench a TREND-RIDER because of a few stop-outs during the open when the whole day prior has been steadily trending (up or down, whatever the case). The established prior-day trend is the signal; the open shakeout is noise.

**Why:** benching the aligned momentum gate right at the open means being FLAT for the real push that follows the shakeout — you miss the recoup.

**How to apply:** on a strongly-trending day (day-bias clearly UP/DOWN from Paris open), when the aligned momentum gate takes a cluster of stops in the 13:30 UTC open window, HOLD it — treat the open flush as a false-breakout shakeout, not a chop-regime flip. Only bench if the trend STRUCTURE breaks (price loses the range / day-bias flips), not on open-window stop-outs alone. Different from a genuine all-day chop where [[router-benching-lessons-0729]] rule-2 (bench all momentum) applies — that's a REGIME read, this is an open-window read.

**Proof (07-30):** benched grind_long on the 13:33 false-breakout flush (day gave back +1002→+522 in 90s at the 27880 highs). Operator overrode: "liven grind back up, the up momentum will continue." Trend resumed to new highs 28060, ER 0.18→0.46, grind rode it +$104 on the chandelier tail and clawed the giveback back. Operator: "lucky we put it back on."

  Every one of the ten worst (-$1,500 to -$4,500) did its damage after 13:30Z. Before the US open they were only 10-280 points offside.
- 2026-10-26 (E112, E113): At the moment of entry those losers looked like the winners on the chart: same kind of leg, same "fresh extreme" reason.
  So the extra care has to come from you being genuinely selective, not from one pattern to avoid.
- 2026-10-26 (E112): Winners are often slow and bumpy: most of them went 25 points against first and the typical one took about 2 hours to pay.
  A trade sitting red is normal; it is not information that the entry was bad.
- 2026-10-26 (E115): Re-entries on a leg that has already paid twice added risk more than profit; the desk now stops at 2 claims per MIL.
- 2026-10-25 (E112 R1): A rule turned into a checklist made things worse: "no climax, so I join" became a reason to enter. Use what you are told here as
  judgment about when to WAIT, never as a box that, once ticked, makes an entry right.
